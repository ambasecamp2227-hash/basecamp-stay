Frequently Asked Questions

What makes A2 Ghee different?

A2 ghee is made from the milk of Desi cows that produce only A2 beta-casein protein. It is easier to digest and doesn't cause the bloating often associated with regular A1 milk products.

Why is it called "Bilona"?

Bilona is the traditional churning method. Unlike commercial ghee made from cream, Bilona ghee is made from curd, preserving more nutrients and the authentic "danedar" (granular) texture.
